# Kyeot

**Hand work to your own AI agent, and build the screens you need just by asking.**

Kyeot is a phone app plus a small server that runs on your own Mac or Linux computer.
Your agent works on your computer; you talk to it from your phone.

- **Ask for a screen, get a tab.** "Make me a to-do tab" turns into a tab with tables, charts and numbers. Change it by asking, and roll back anytime.
- **Answer when your agent asks.** Hand off work in chat and approve or deny from your phone.
- **Your data stays on your server.** No developer-run server, no analytics, no ad SDKs.
